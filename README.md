## Featured projects

- **[10-K disclosure drift](https://neifertg.github.io/10k-drift-detector/)** — Compares Item 1A (Risk Factors) and Item 9A (Controls and Procedures) across consecutive Form 10-Ks, then compares those changes between peers in six sectors, including Walmart and Costco. Every finding on the company and peer pages quotes the filing sentence it came from and links to that filing. If a comparison is not supported, the report says so and does not fill the gap. ([Repo](https://github.com/neifertg/10k-drift-detector))
- **[Risk & Audit Analytics Portfolio](https://github.com/neifertg/risk-analytics-portfolio)** — Working audit-analytics tools and source-citing assistants, with live demos and honest write-ups.
- **[Audit knowledge graph](https://neifertg.github.io/audit-knowledge-graph/)** — Reconciles a synthetic bank's ERM register and Internal Audit matrices into one map, then asks the same planning questions in a graph and in SQL. The brief shows coverage candidates, repeat issues under different titles, shared-system exposure, and concentrated control ownership. The answers matched in both languages. It labels planted test cases and does not score risk or set the plan. Synthetic data only; not any employer's data. ([Repo](https://github.com/neifertg/audit-knowledge-graph))
- **[ML Field Guide](https://github.com/neifertg/ml-field-guide)** — 19 machine-learning techniques explained for readers without a statistics background.
- **[capitan](https://github.com/neifertg/capitan)** — A plan-first harness for building with AI coding agents.
